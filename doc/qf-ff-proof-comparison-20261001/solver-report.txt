## Protocol

The public FMCAD 2026 artifact is https://zenodo.org/records/20133205. Its selected list contains 408 paths, deduplicated to 390 SHA-256-verified original inputs; 10 seconds whole-worker wall time per input/configuration, four workers, deterministic shuffle, 16 GiB sampled process-tree RSS cap. The prior FMCAD runner is reused. Its repository-root path was adjusted, and a receipt-reading fix was applied after an interrupted worker left truncated JSON: all 2,247 completed measurements were preserved, one incomplete attempt was archived and retried, and the remaining 2,043 runs resumed. See `resume-audit.json` for exact runner hashes and the affected input. No completed measurement was replaced. All displayed results are fresh runs in one campaign. Python startup and solver startup are included. The source corpus and solver flags are unchanged. No Lean-SMT or proof-production runs are included. Timing is a concurrent throughput screen on a non-reserved host, not an isolated latency measurement.

Latest main was built natively with CoCoA, production optimization, GMP and static linking; unrelated LibPoly support is disabled. Release builds use their packaged arithmetic dependencies. Executable SHA-256 hashes, exact versions, configurations and source/build provenance are archived. The initial smoke test of non-GPL binaries lacked CoCoA and is excluded.

## Interpretation

The corpus is the artifact's UNSAT selection: counts measure completed UNSAT answers, not independently checked certificates. The known cvc5 split false-UNSAT issue on a different satisfiable input is not adjudicated by this corpus. SAT answers, errors and resource exhaustion are reported separately in `summary.json`, never counted as successes. Historical results are not substituted for fresh measurements.

The mixed array/sequence examples are a separate generated functional check; they do not enter this timing corpus. See `mixed-examples/README.md`.

Full per-input results: `per-input.csv`. Shared/exclusive successes: `overlap.json`. Raw solver output, exit statuses, timings and resource receipts: `runs/`.
