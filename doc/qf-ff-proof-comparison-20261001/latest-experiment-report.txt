Retained implementation
Optional --boolean-backend circuits with --backend native. Schema version 4
rebuilds candidate lemmas and definitions from the original input. Mux, XOR,
AND and complement facts use small local truth tables, each certified with
ordinary PAC rules. Signed wire regrouping retains its defining equalities as
premises. Shared arithmetic DAG matching is memoized; unsupported deep forms
are declined. No new trusted rule, increased budget or skipped checker.
The existing automatic backend policy and explicit ranges mode are unchanged.

Measurement contract
FMCAD26 FF_UNSAT_SMT, 390 distinct inputs / 408 member paths. Deadline includes
worker startup, production, independent replay, standalone FFPacheck, and
Carcara invoking FFPacheck again. Frozen native binary is unchanged from the
previous run. All source/binary/checker hashes and per-stage receipts retained.
Full sweeps used four sequential batches of 100/100/100/90 inputs; each batch
used four workers. Completed bundles were compressed and hash-verified between
batches, outside measured runs, to bound disk usage.
